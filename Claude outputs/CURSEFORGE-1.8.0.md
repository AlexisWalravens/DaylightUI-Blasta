# Daylight Blasta 1.8.0

A big update for the group finder, new looks for RestedXP and the world map, and money that reads the same everywhere.

## Dungeon finder
- **Groups and solo players side by side.** Find a group opens wider and shows groups on the left, solo players on the right. Drag the window narrower and it switches back to one column on the fly. The width is remembered; right-click the resize grip to reset it.
- **Filters open by themselves** when you open Find a group on Dungeons (closed by hand, they stay closed until next time).
- **Pick several dungeons at once.** The list stays open while you tick them.
- **New filter "Already in the group":** roles and classes a group must already have, several at once.
- **Level ranges** beside every dungeon and listing, colored by your level.
- **One-click apply:** an "Apply" button on a group's card whispers the leader your level, class, roles and dungeon. Shift-click to edit the note first, or set your own with `/blasta apply <text>`. "Whisper" is still there for a blank whisper.
- Solo players who can fill a picked open role now show instead of being hidden.
- The group card matches the rest of the window (no lighter background).
- German: the playstyle "Ziehhilfe" is now "Mitziehen".

## Tooltips and money
- **DaylightUI's coins everywhere:** Auctionator's prices, the game's money boxes (merchant, mail, quests) and Auctionator's auction house lists.
- **Sell price per piece**, like Auctionator's prices. Hold Shift for the whole stack (follows Auctionator's "Shift for stack price" setting).

## RestedXP Guides
- **New "Blasta Elegant" look:** no boxes or heavy backgrounds, just thin lines between header, steps and footer, an accent bar beside the current steps, shadowed text and no logo. On by default (Game window skins → Other addons).

## World map
- **Boat and zeppelin routes:** hover a boat or zeppelin symbol to see its route as a gold line, with an arrow where it leaves the map. Continent and world maps get small dock symbols to hover. Needs DaylightUI's "Boats and zeppelins".

## More
- **Training page:** a new total for everything still ahead, beside "Learn now costs".
- **Shadow Word: Death helper:** the marker now leans with slanted health bars.
- **Info panel:** the exact gold amount is now opt-in, since it got cut off under the minimap.
- **Zone title:** "Discovered", "Resting" and some other texts now show in German.
- **Addon list:** Blasta shows in its blue-to-lime colors.

## Fixes
- Fixed an error with "Frame shape" when DaylightUI's corner setting was empty.
- Angled nameplates no longer depend on DaylightUI's border setting being set.
